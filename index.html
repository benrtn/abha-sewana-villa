Meet Sriyani

A year ago I opened the doors of my house to travellers. It was not a hotel then and it still is not one today — it is the home where my family lives, on a quiet street in Anuradhapura, with a garden and a veranda where the afternoons go by slowly.

I cook every meal myself. I use the clay pots my mother used, because a curry cooked in clay tastes of something a steel pan simply cannot give it. In the morning I make hoppers and lentil curry; in the evening I lay the table on the veranda and we eat together.

Some guests come with me to the market to choose the vegetables. Some cook beside me. Some just sit in the garden with a cup of tea and say nothing at all. Whatever you feel like doing — my house is open to you.

Sriyani
