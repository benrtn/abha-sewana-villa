At the villa

Sri Lankan cooking experience

You start where I start: chopping, grinding the spices, deciding how much chilli your table can take. Then we cook it in the clay pots, on the open fire, the way it has been done in this house for a long time.

When everything is ready we sit down and eat it together. Most people leave knowing how to make at least one curry properly.

At the villa

Traditional dinner on the veranda

In the evening I lay the long table on the veranda, with the garden dark and cool in front of you. Rice in the middle, and every curry in its own clay pot so you can taste them one by one.

Dhal, jackfruit, pumpkin, egg curry, coconut sambol — whatever the market had that morning. Tell me in advance if you do not eat meat, or if chilli is not for you.

At the villa

Local market tour

Come with me in the morning to the market in town. I will show you the vegetables you have never seen before, let you smell the spices, and haggle a little so you can see how it is done.

Whatever we carry home is what you eat that night.

At the villa

Handicraft workshop

Some evenings we clear the table and make something with our hands — simple traditional crafts, nothing you need any skill for.

It is a good, slow way to spend an evening with the other travellers staying in the house.

At the villa

Sri Lankan breakfast

Breakfast is hoppers straight from the pan, lentil curry, coconut sambol and whatever fruit is ripe — papaya, banana, mango when it is the season.

It is served in clay bowls on the veranda, with plain tea or coffee, from early in the morning.

Around Anuradhapura

Tours we can arrange for you

Sacred City of Anuradhapura

The old capital is ten minutes away by tuk-tuk. Two thousand years of dagobas, monasteries and bathing pools, best seen early in the morning before the heat arrives.

Ruwanwelisaya · Jaya Sri Maha Bodhi · Thuparamaya · Abhayagiri · Isurumuniya · Kuttam Pokuna

Wilpattu National Park safari

A full day in Sri Lanka's largest national park, about two hours from the house. Leopards, elephants, sloth bears and the natural lakes the park is named after. We arrange the jeep and the driver; you leave before dawn.
