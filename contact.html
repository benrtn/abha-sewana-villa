The last hundred metres

Finding us

Look for the white gate and the green sign with the name of the villa on it. That is the landmark. Once you can see the green sign, you have arrived.

Tuk-tuk drivers sometimes do not know the house number, so show them this photo, or call us and let us speak to the driver.

Travel

Getting here

Anuradhapura is easy to reach from almost anywhere on the island. Tell us how you are travelling and we will help with the last part.

  • Anuradhapura railway station

    About 10 minutes by tuk-tuk. Trains run from Colombo Fort several times a day; the slow morning train is the pretty one.

  • Bus from Colombo

    Direct buses leave Colombo Pettah all day and take around five hours. Get off at Anuradhapura New Bus Stand and call us. We will tell your driver the way.

  • Bus from Kandy or Dambulla

    Around three hours from Kandy, and about an hour and a half from Dambulla. An easy stop to add if you are coming down from Sigiriya.

  • Sigiriya airport

    The nearest domestic airport is about 70 km away, roughly an hour and a half by road. Tell us your landing time and we will have a driver waiting.

Arriving late, or with children and a lot of luggage? Call us before you travel and we will arrange a driver to meet you.
